Flash UNDERGROWTH

Install the experimental 0.1.0-hw1 build from Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. The browser uses USB MIDI with SysEx permission. This build passed the software gate but has not been tried on a physical FM1.

2. Prepare your FM1

  1. Export your Caravel projects and settings, then make and verify a full flash backup. The installer does not create one.
  2. Keep your Caravel 0.6.0 package and checksum available for recovery.
  3. Connect the FM1 directly with a USB data cable. Close DAWs and other MIDI tabs.

Chrome may ask for MIDI access, including SysEx. Identifying the device does not start an update.

No device checked.

3. Install

Installation can erase data from the current firmware. Keep the USB cable and power connected until the FM1 restarts and this page reports the new identity. Direct installation from Caravel 0.6.0 has not yet been physically tested.

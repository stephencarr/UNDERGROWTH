Perform without losing your place
HOME mixes: four knobs are track levels, first four lower keys are mutes. REC arms and shows Hits / Motion / Both; KNOB1 chooses. Leave for EDIT, ENV or FX to perform, then REC finishes in place. Hold HOME for Panic.

Control map, session limits and research

SELECT turns pages; repeated EDIT, ENV or FX also advances their pages. Each track remembers its editing page and selected step. SEL auditions the selected draft or SEQ step without writing a trig. HOME’s SELECT pages offer recovery and a key guide. In HOME and step-entry SEQ, upper keys 1–4 choose tracks, 5 copies a step, 6 pastes, 7 is Undo/redo, 8 rejoins locks, 9 auditions, and 10–11 change lock pages. Copy/paste works in SEQ on the same organism. In Hits/Both recording, SEQ returns the full keyboard to playing notes. Mixing has separate session state, outside locks, motion and score Undo. Mutes stop new dry/send output; shared delay and reverb tails can continue. Reload loses the session. SAVE, PRESETS, ARP and LFO remain unavailable. The screen is a display, not a touchscreen. Browser and firmware share the C controller; physical playing and CPU/recovery validation remain outstanding. Read the workflow design and research. 03 / Seed a rhythm

Leave room for something.

Choose a step, enable Note on, then set pitch and gate. Unchecked steps are rests. Gate over 100% overlaps the next step; Slide glides from a still-sounding note. Body adds weight, Edge adds harmonics, Length shapes release.
